# Online appendices

Supplementary material for "Pricing the energy transition: Retail tariff designs for the Single Buyer models in high renewable power systems".

- [`appendix-B-theoretical-model.pdf`](appendix-B-theoretical-model.pdf): Online Appendix B. The full theoretical model of retail tariff design in a single-buyer market (assumptions, cost of supply, welfare, tariff regimes and the cost-causation benchmark) and the Nomenclature.
- [`appendix-C-analytical-results.pdf`](appendix-C-analytical-results.pdf): Online Appendix C. The formal statements and proofs of the lemmas and propositions on the cost of supply, the loss measures, the revenue shortfall, the welfare ordering, the cross-subsidy under the flat rate and the DSS+CPP limit.
- [`appendix-D2-thermal-merit-order.pdf`](appendix-D2-thermal-merit-order.pdf): Online Appendix D.2. The thermal merit order of the Saudi calibration: the three thermal blocks, their heat rates, the calibration of the block variable costs and the distribution of the first-best shadow cost (Table D.4).
- [`appendix-D6-com-load-pattern.pdf`](appendix-D6-com-load-pattern.pdf): Online Appendix D.6. The load pattern of commercial, government and other consumers: its shape and the reasons for it, the cross-checks against regional and system data, the load factors and the alternative patterns run as sensitivities (Figures D.1 and D.2).
- [`appendix-E-simulation-method.pdf`](appendix-E-simulation-method.pdf): Online Appendix E. The simulation method: the calendar and reference demand, supply, dispatch and shadow costs, the first best, the tariff regimes and their calibration rules, the searches behind the optimised classes and the status marks, the capacity standard, the event hours, the welfare and incidence measures, the adequacy screen and the comparison of the rules with the optimised classes (Figure E.1).
- [`appendix-F-sensitivity-analysis.pdf`](appendix-F-sensitivity-analysis.pdf): Online Appendix F. The sensitivity analysis: the runs and their losses (Tables F.1 and F.2, Figure F.1), transmission and distribution losses (Table F.3), the timing of renewable output (Figure F.2), the merit order and the consumption tiers (Table F.4), the allocation of costs among consumer groups (Table F.5) and the capacity channel of critical-peak pricing.

Each appendix says at the top which of its references are to the paper and which are to the other online appendices.

Lean and Claude Code were used to check the theorems and proofs, and Claude Code to check the code and to revise the text.
